About Mt. Masaraga

Mt. Masaraga is one of Albay's protected landscapes known for its rich biodiversity, scenic trails, and breathtaking summit views. The Protected Area Management Office requires all hikers to secure an official climbing permit before entering the mountain.

Permit Requirements

Government ID

Submit a valid government-issued ID.

Medical Certificate

Required for verifying physical fitness.

Waiver Form

Agree to the hiking terms and conditions.

Environmental Fee

Pay the required conservation fee.

Safety Reminders

  • Register before entering the protected area.
  • Bring enough drinking water.
  • Leave No Trace.
  • Follow the assigned trail guide.
  • Respect wildlife and protected habitats.
